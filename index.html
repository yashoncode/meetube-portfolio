About this app

Ad-free playback

Watch without ads. Skip sponsor segments with SponsorBlock and keep watching in picture-in-picture.

Music with MeeTune

Follow synced lyrics or play music with your screen locked. Try singing along with AI Karaoke (beta). It removes vocals right on your phone.

Offline downloads

Save videos in up to 4K to your chosen folder. Smart downloads remember your quality setting.

MeeTube TV

Watch ad-free on Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV devices running Fire OS. Browse with your remote and press OK to play. Sign in with a code to see your subscriptions, or watch without an account.
